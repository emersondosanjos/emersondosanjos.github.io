# Home surface (src/pages/index.astro) and site shell

Scope: home page plus the shared shell used by Research, Publications, Open science and CV. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport). Audience: researchers/reviewers and industry partners, equally. Job: reach a work with its DOI, code and data, or a reason to get in touch. Constraint: papers under review show topic and status only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A hydrogen career read as a hydrogen emission spectrum. The spectroscopic plate is the page's structure and its timeline; it refuses the photo-plus-bio-column academic template and the near-black-with-neon-glow default.

OWN-WORLD: Deep spectral ultramarine ground (#0D1B5E) with a darker plate strip (#06103D) and pale calibration paper sections (#EAF0FF) for reading. Four Balmer colours are the only accents, each owning one research line: Hδ 410 violet, Hγ 434 indigo-blue, Hβ 486 cyan, Hα 656 red. Lines are crisp, flat, no glow, no gradients. Wavelength rulers, tick scales and fixed-width mono readouts frame every list.

STORY: The visitor sees ten years of work as emission lines, learns there are four series (reforming, transport, electrolysis, recovery), opens any line to get the paper, DOI, code and data, and finds where to cite or write.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: thin text nav and calibration ruler. Left-top: name at display scale in light weight, two lines. Under it one sentence. Lower 40%: full-bleed spectral plate, lines placed by year (2016–2026 across the width), coloured by series, intensity by status; a slit readout shows the selected line. Primary action: the plate itself plus "Open code & data".

FORM: Spectroscopic plate as timeline; candidate 1 on my ordered list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the user). seed:0f78572499ea68d3

SIGNATURE: A slit cursor scrubs the plate; the nearest line snaps into a spectrometer readout (λ, year, venue, links); arrow keys step lines; the series legend filters lines. Lines emit in chronological order on load; reduced motion shows the plate static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

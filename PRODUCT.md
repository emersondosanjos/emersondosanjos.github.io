## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (user answer):
- Researchers, reviewers and editors who need to find a paper, its data and code (Zenodo records, GitHub repositories) and cite them correctly.
- Industry partners and collaborators in hydrogen and electrolysis R&D who want to understand what the researcher builds and measures, and how to work with him.

## Product Purpose

The personal research site of Emerson Barbosa dos Anjos, in English. It gathers all his work over time and is the front door to an open-access environment of code, data and design files kept on GitHub and Zenodo. Success: a visitor reaches the right paper, dataset or repository in a few clicks and leaves with a correct citation or a reason to get in touch.

## Positioning

A project-style research site in the register of open-source CFD project sites such as OpenFOAM and openfuelcell2, rather than a generic CV template. It is built around one continuous research line, hydrogen production, from reactor modelling (steam reforming of toluene, membrane reactors, solar thermochemical reactors) to heat and mass transfer (droplet freezing, membrane distillation) to the membraneless alkaline electrolyzer series of the doctorate.

## Operating Context

- Content changes often until the papers in review are published: statuses move from "in preparation" to "under review" to "published", DOIs and Zenodo records are added. The publication list must live in one data file that the author can edit without touching layout.
- Data of papers under review must never be public. The site may name a paper under review only by topic and status, without results or figures.
- Zenodo community: "Emerson dos Anjos – Membraneless alkaline electrolyzer series", https://zenodo.org/communities/dosanjos-membraneless-electrolyzer.
- GitHub: account `emersondosanjos`; public repository `Membraneless-Electrolyzer` (paper 01 design files, CC BY 4.0).
